Tradeski and Neural Canvas#

These two make engineering claims rather than research ones: Tradeski's indicators are hand-coded on NumPy and covered by its CI (Python and JavaScript suites); Neural Canvas runs MediaPipe's 21-landmark hand model on-device at roughly 60 frames a second. Both are live and can be checked directly.
